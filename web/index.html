You play White. Select one of your pieces and press Ask for a tip to hear from that piece, or ask with nothing selected and someone volunteers. Each piece has a hidden reliability, and a tip is graded when you play it. Black's pieces taunt you and sometimes offer tips. Don't trust those.

The pieces remember. Within a game they notice when you ignore them or leave them hanging. With Remember past games on, they carry their records and grudges into the next game. The browser saves that history. When model voices are enabled, relevant memories may be sent with a line request. Turning Chatty off keeps dialogue to key events and requested tips.

With Model voices on, Claude writes one line per beat live, from what happened and what that piece remembers, using your own Claude usage. Lines marked ✦ came from the model. The game still decides every move and every event, and the pieces fall back to their scripted lines whenever the model is slow or unavailable.

The quick engine grades each tip when you play it. House rules automatically draw after threefold repetition, 50 moves per side without a pawn move or capture, or insufficient material detected by the engine.
